THE IDEA BEHIND NAZM

Read across languages.
Keep the differences.

Put a ghazal beside a song and see what you notice. An image might carry both lines. A repeated phrase might change its weight each time it returns. The resemblance is a place to begin reading.

These essays bring Persian and Urdu literary terms into conversation with English poetry and music. A useful comparison still needs room for context: who is speaking, what the words meant there, and what a translation leaves behind.

A SMALL EXPERIMENT

Change the sentence.

One thought, three ways to write it.
